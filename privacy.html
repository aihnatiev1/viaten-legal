Privacy Policy

Effective October 17, 2026

ViaTen ("the app", "we") is built so that your personal reflections stay yours.

What we collect The app has no accounts, no advertising and no tracking, and no servers that receive your content.

Anonymous usage statistics. To learn whether the app helps people, it sends a small set of anonymous events through TelemetryDeck, a privacy-focused analytics provider: onboarding started/completed, a session started/completed (with its day number), a premium preview started/completed, the subscription screen shown (and where), and a trial or subscription started, plus whether the device has Premium. Events use a random identifier that TelemetryDeck hashes and that is not linked to your identity; IP addresses are not stored. They never include your reflections, notes, highlights, which journeys or passages you read, or search terms. You can turn this off at any time in You › Share anonymous usage statistics.

What stays on your device Your reflections, notes, highlights, saved verses, reading progress and settings are stored on your iPhone. If you turn on iCloud for the app, Apple stores this data in your private iCloud database, which only you can access; private text such as reflections is encrypted by Apple before it leaves your device. We cannot see this data.

Purchases Subscriptions are processed by Apple. The app only learns from Apple whether a subscription is active. We never receive your name, email or payment details.

Notifications Daily reminders are scheduled locally on your iPhone. No push service or token is used.

Diagnostics If you choose to share analytics with app developers in iOS Settings, Apple may provide us aggregated, anonymous usage and crash statistics. You control this in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

Your choices - Export: You › Export my data creates a file you can save or share. - Delete: You › Delete all my data removes everything from this iPhone. Deleting the app also removes local data. iCloud data can be removed in Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Storage.

Children The app is not directed to children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Changes If this policy changes, we will update the effective date and, for material changes, tell you in the app.
